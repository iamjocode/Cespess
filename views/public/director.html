Our Director

Meet Our Director

Gloria Chukwudebe is a professor of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. She has several publications and has presented many papers at National and International Conferences.

She has also been an invited Guest Speaker for several organizations in Nigeria. Gloria teaches courses in electronic & computer engineering up to postgraduate level. She was Head of Department, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, FUTO, 1999 – 2005.

Gloria enjoys coaching and mentoring young people. She is married with five children. She has a passion for books on personal development. Gloria is an active IEEE Volunteer and the current – Chair for IEEE Nigeria Section. Her favourite magazine is IEEE Spectrum. To thaw out, she watches Nigerian comedies and local soaps- Papa Ajasco, ‘It is a Super Story’, etc.

Prof. Gloria A. C
