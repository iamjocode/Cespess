Environmental Standard

Evironmental Standards | by Prof. Amadi | Google classroom

Overview FAQ

Objective

The main aim of Environmental Standards executive education programmes is to produce competent, intellectually mature, ethical and socially responsible environmental resource management professionals who can assess, manage and monitor the environmental aspects of organizations, paticipate in multidisciplinary project work, as well as develop critical thinking, problems solving and research skills.

Mode of Delivery

The curriculum will be taught through classroom contact, online, multimedia resources and presentation, interactive sessions, capestone case studies and participant feedback mechanism.

Course Progression

Credits obtained from modules taken in Executive Education or Short Course program may be deployed towards partial fulfulment for the requirement of awards of Advanced Diploma

Duration

Executive Short Courses (5days)
Advanced Certificate (9months)

Examination/Assessment

A certificate of partijcipation is awarded on completion of any executive or short course, whereas, any candidate who completes all the mandatory executives modules, assignments, field work/capstone project and examinations will be awarded an Advanced Diploma Cerftificate.
